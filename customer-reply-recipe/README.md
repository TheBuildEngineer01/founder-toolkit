# The Customer Reply Recipe (5 habits that lose customers, and what to do instead)

**Most customers don't leave because of price. They leave because nobody answered.**

In a May 2026 US survey of 1,002 small-business customers, **36% said they would not hire a small business again because it was hard to reach or slow to respond**, the top reason ahead of professionalism and quality ([HoneyBook / The Harris Poll](https://news.honeybook.com/news/honeybook-research-poor-communication-top-reason-customers-leave-small-businesses)).

---

## Step 1: Check your kitchen (tick what happens in your business)

| Bad ingredient | What it looks like | Happens to us? |
|----------------|--------------------|:--------------:|
| 1 cup slow reply | Messages answered hours later, or next morning | ☐ |
| A pinch of "Seen ✓✓" | Customer sees you read it, but no answer comes | ☐ |
| 2 tbsp "kal bataata hoon" | "I'll tell you tomorrow", then nobody follows up | ☐ |
| 1 "send account number" loop | Payment takes 4–5 messages and a screenshot | ☐ |
| Garnish: zero follow-up | Interested customers go quiet and you never check back | ☐ |

**2 or more ticks:** you're losing orders you already earned.

---

## Step 2: The new recipe

| Instead of… | Do this |
|-------------|---------|
| Slow reply | An instant first reply, even at night (auto reply or AI agent) |
| "Seen" with no answer | Answer the top 10 questions (price, delivery, sizes, timings) from a ready list |
| "Kal bataata hoon" | Give the answer now, or a clear time: "I'll confirm by 3 PM today" |
| Account number loop | One message with the total + a payment link or all payment details |
| Zero follow-up | One polite follow-up after a few hours, then stop |

---

## Step 3: Ready-to-use replies (adapt and save as quick replies)

**Instant first reply (after hours):**
> Hi! Thanks for your message 🙂 Here are our prices and delivery details: [link/photo]. If you'd like to order, reply with the item and city and we'll confirm first thing in the morning.

**Price question:**
> [Product] is Rs [price]. Sizes [S–XL] in stock. Delivery to [city] is Rs [x], [2–3] days. Want me to book one for you?

**Instead of "kal bataata hoon":**
> Let me check stock and confirm by [time] today.

**Payment (one message):**
> Your total is Rs [x] including delivery. Pay here: [link]. Or COD is available. Once done, your order is confirmed ✅

**One polite follow-up:**
> Hi [name]! Just checking if you'd still like the [product]. It's in stock. Happy to help with any question.

*Only message people who contacted you first, follow WhatsApp's Business and Commerce policies, and stop after one follow-up if there's no reply.*
