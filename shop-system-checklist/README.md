# From Notebook to System: Shop Checklist

**Your shop deserves a system, not a notebook.**

Many shops still run on a register: stock in one notebook, udhaar (customer credit) in another, and the day's sales added up with a calculator after closing. This checklist helps you see what a simple shop system would fix, and what to prepare before you get one.

---

## 5 problems a simple system fixes

| # | Problem | On paper | With a system |
|---|---------|----------|---------------|
| 1 | **Stock you can't count** | You find out an item is finished when a customer asks for it | Live stock count, with a low-stock alert |
| 2 | **Udhaar in a register** | Who owes what? Pages of crossed-out lines | Each customer's balance on one screen, with reminders |
| 3 | **Sales added up at night** | Totalling the day with a calculator after closing | Today's total updates with every sale |
| 4 | **Profit is a guess** | You know your sales, not what you actually earned | Profit per item and per day, worked out for you |
| 5 | **Shop stops when you leave** | Nobody else can check stock or prices | Staff logins, and you can check it all from your phone |

---

## Quick check: is it time?

Tick what's true for your shop:

- [ ] You've run out of an item without noticing in the last month
- [ ] You keep udhaar in a notebook or on loose pages
- [ ] Closing the day takes more than 15 minutes of counting
- [ ] You're not sure which products make you the most profit
- [ ] Staff have to call you to check a price or stock

**3 or more ticks:** a simple system will save you time and money every week.

---

## Before you get a system, prepare

1. **Your product list:** name, buying price, selling price, current stock
2. **Your udhaar list:** customer name, phone, amount owed
3. **Who uses it:** just you, or staff too?
4. **How you sell:** counter only, WhatsApp orders, delivery, or all three
5. **What you want on your phone** every evening (sales, profit, low stock, money owed)

Having these ready makes setup much faster.
