# The 2 AM Test

**Can a customer buy from you at 2 AM without waking you up?** A 10-minute self-check for small businesses that sell on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or a website.

People don't only shop 9 to 5, and they don't like waiting: in one 2026 survey, **70% of consumers said they expect a business to reply within an hour** ([EZ Texting, 2026 Consumer Texting Behavior Report](https://eztexting.com/report/2026-consumer-texting-behavior-report), 959 US consumers). This test shows which steps of your sale still need *you* awake.

---

## Part A: Take the test

Pick the answer that is honestly true for your business today. Give yourself **0 marks for (a), 1 for (b), 2.5 for (c)**.

**Q1. A customer asks the price at 2 AM. Who answers?**
- [ ] (a) Me, the next morning
- [ ] (b) A generic "we'll get back to you" auto-message
- [ ] (c) An auto-reply or AI agent that sends the price, photos and an order link

**Q2. They want to pay right now. How?**
- [ ] (a) They wait for me to send account details
- [ ] (b) They transfer and send a screenshot for me to check later
- [ ] (c) A payment link or online checkout. Paid in seconds

**Q3. "Is this in stock?"**
- [ ] (a) I check the shelf or godown and reply later
- [ ] (b) I guess from memory
- [ ] (c) Stock shows on the website / app and updates itself on every sale

**Q4. Next day: "Where is my order?"**
- [ ] (a) They call or message me, and I find out
- [ ] (b) I search through old chats to find it
- [ ] (c) A tracking message sends itself when the order ships

**Your score: ____ / 10**

---

## Part B: What your score means

| Score | Result | What to do first |
|-------|--------|------------------|
| 0 – 3 | Your phone is the night shift | Start with Q1: an automatic reply with prices and an order link |
| 4 – 7 | Customers are leaving quietly | Fix the step where people drop off (usually payment or stock) |
| 8 – 10 | Top of the class | Add a morning summary of overnight sales and look at reorder reminders |

---

## Part C: What each (c) answer needs

| Step | What to set up | Good to know |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| Replies | Auto-replies or an AI agent on WhatsApp Business / Instagram / website chat | Give it your real prices, delivery areas and FAQs. Let it hand tricky chats to you |
| Ordering | A catalog or simple website with an order button | Even a one-page store works if it's clear |
| Payment | Payment links or online checkout | Send the link with the order confirmation |
| Stock | Stock that updates on every sale | One source of truth, not a notebook and a sheet |
| Tracking | Automatic shipping / tracking message | Send it the moment the order is booked with the courier |
| Overview | A morning summary to your phone | What sold, what's pending, what's low |

**Tip:** don't automate everything at once. Fix your lowest-scoring question first, test it for a week, then move to the next.
