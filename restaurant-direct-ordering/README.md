# Restaurant Direct Ordering System

A practical plan for restaurants, cafes and cloud kitchens that want **repeat customers to order directly** (WhatsApp + your own order page) instead of paying delivery-app commission on every order.

> Keep the delivery apps for *new* customers. Bring the customers who already love your food back to a direct channel.

## Why this matters

- Delivery apps in Pakistan charge restaurants a commission on every order; Dawn reports an average of around 25%, varying with order volume.
- On a Rs 1,000 order, that is roughly Rs 250 per order, including orders from people who were already your regulars.
- Restaurants have also asked delivery platforms to share customer data; on the app, the customer relationship usually stays with the platform.

## The system (4 parts)

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **1. WhatsApp AI ordering agent** | Answers "menu bhej dein", shares the menu and deals, takes the order, confirms the address and total, 24/7 |
| **2. Your own order page** | A simple link (menu, cart, COD / JazzCash / Easypaisa) you share on Instagram, WhatsApp status, receipts and the parcel |
| **3. Kitchen screen** | Every confirmed order appears instantly for the kitchen; status updates go to the customer automatically |
| **4. Repeat-customer offers** | A weekly message to past customers (their favourite dish, a small direct-order deal) so they come back directly |
| *Owner report* | Daily orders, direct vs app share, top dishes, repeat customers |

## Step-by-step setup checklist

1. **Get a WhatsApp Business number** for orders (not the owner's personal number).
2. **Write your menu once** as structured data: item, price, options, availability.
3. **Set up the AI agent** to: greet, send the menu, take items and quantities, confirm address and total, then hand the confirmed order to the kitchen.
4. **Launch your order page** with the same menu (one source of truth for prices).
5. **Connect the kitchen screen** (a tablet is enough): new order alert, prepare, ready, dispatched.
6. **Automate customer updates**: "order received", "being prepared", "rider on the way".
7. **Ask for opt-in** before sending offers ("Reply YES to get weekly deals"), and always allow "STOP".
8. **Weekly repeat message**: favourite dish + one simple direct-order offer.
9. **Put the direct link everywhere**: Instagram bio, WhatsApp status, menu cards, a sticker inside every app parcel.
10. **Track for 30 days** (table below) and adjust offers.

## Message templates (Roman Urdu, edit to your brand)

**Welcome / menu**
> Assalam o Alaikum! [Restaurant] mein khush aamdeed. Menu yeh raha: [link]. Kya order karna chahenge?

**Order confirmation**
> Aap ka order: [items]. Total: Rs [amount]. Address: [address]. Confirm karne ke liye "Confirm" likhein.

**Status update**
> Aap ka order tayyar ho raha hai. Rider [time] mein pohanch jayega.

**Weekly repeat offer (opted-in customers only)**
> [Name], aap ki favourite [dish] is hafte direct order par [offer]. Order ke liye reply karein. (STOP likhein agar messages nahi chahiye)

## Good practice

- Do not undercut your app listing so hard that the platform penalises you; check your contract terms.
- Only message customers who gave you their number for orders and agreed to offers.
- Keep prices identical across WhatsApp and the order page.
- Measure **direct share %** every week; that is the number this system is built to grow.
