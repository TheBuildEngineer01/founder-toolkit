# Outgrowing WhatsApp: Checklist

**Keep WhatsApp for talking. Use a system for running the business.**

WhatsApp is great for talking to customers. But when orders, customer history, follow-ups and sales numbers all live inside chats, things start slipping. This checklist helps you see whether your business has outgrown running on chats, and what a simple system needs to include.

---

## 5 signs you've outgrown it

| # | Sign | What it looks like | The fix |
|---|------|--------------------|---------|
| 1 | **Orders get lost in old chats** | Scrolling up to find what a customer ordered last week | Every order saved in one place, searchable |
| 2 | **No customer history** | Every repeat customer feels like a stranger | Each customer's full history on one screen |
| 3 | **Nobody knows who replied** | Two people answer the same customer, or nobody does | Each chat assigned to one person, with a status |
| 4 | **Follow-ups live in your memory** | Quotes go out. Then silence. | Automatic follow-up reminders, so nothing slips |
| 5 | **You can't see the numbers** | How many inquiries became sales this month? No idea. | A simple dashboard: inquiries, sales, what's pending |

**Score:** count the signs that sound familiar.
- **0–1:** chats are probably still fine for now.
- **2–3:** start with the one that costs you the most (usually lost orders or missed follow-ups).
- **4–5:** it's time for a simple system.

---

## What a simple system needs (version one)

You don't need a huge CRM. Most small businesses need just these:

- [ ] **Customers:** name, phone, notes, and every past order
- [ ] **Orders / inquiries:** one record each, with a status (new → quoted → confirmed → delivered)
- [ ] **Owner:** who on the team is handling it
- [ ] **Reminders:** follow-up dates that trigger a nudge
- [ ] **Dashboard:** this week's inquiries, sales and pending items

WhatsApp stays as the place you talk. The system is where the information lives.

---

## Before you talk to a developer, note down

1. Roughly how many inquiries or orders you get **per day**
2. How many people on your team **reply to customers**
3. The **steps** an order goes through, from first message to delivery
4. What you'd want to see on **one screen** every morning
