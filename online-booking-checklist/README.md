# Online Booking Checklist: Salons, Clinics, Gyms & Tutors

**Let customers book themselves. You do the work you're good at.**

If every booking still comes through a phone call or a long chat, you're losing customers you never hear about. This checklist shows what phone-only booking costs you, and what a simple online booking system needs.

---

## 5 things phone-only booking costs you

| # | Cost | By phone | With online booking |
|---|------|----------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Missed calls, missed bookings** | You're busy with a client, the phone rings, they book elsewhere | Customers book themselves, any time, from one link |
| 2 | **Double bookings** | Two people, same slot, one unhappy customer | Only free slots are shown, so clashes can't happen |
| 3 | **No-shows** | An empty slot you could have filled | Automatic reminders before every appointment |
| 4 | **Endless back-and-forth** | "Is 4 free? No? 5? 6?" Ten messages for one booking | The customer picks a time in one tap |
| 5 | **No record of regulars** | No history of visits or what they had done | Customer history, and reminders to come back |

---

## What a simple booking system needs (version one)

- [ ] **Booking link** you can share on WhatsApp, Instagram and Google
- [ ] **Services list** with duration and price
- [ ] **Staff / room calendars** so the right person's free slots show
- [ ] **Reminders** by WhatsApp, SMS or email before the appointment
- [ ] **Reschedule / cancel** link, so customers don't need to call
- [ ] **Customer history:** visits, notes, what they had done
- [ ] **Owner dashboard:** today's bookings, no-shows, busiest days
- [ ] *(Optional)* deposits or online payment to cut no-shows

---

## Before you talk to a developer, note down

1. Your **services**, how long each takes, and the price
2. **How many staff** (or rooms, courts, tutors) take bookings
3. Your **opening hours** and breaks
4. How customers book **today** (calls, WhatsApp, walk-ins)
5. Your **cancellation rule** (e.g. how much notice you need)
