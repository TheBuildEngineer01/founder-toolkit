# Night-Shift WhatsApp Checklist for Online Sellers

Customers message at 11:40 PM. Most shops reply the next morning, and by then the customer may have ordered somewhere else. This checklist helps you cover the night shift, starting with free settings and moving up to an AI agent.

## 1. Measure your night gap (10 minutes)

Scroll back through the last 7 days of chats and note:

| Question | Your number |
|---|---|
| How many customer messages arrived after 9 PM? | |
| How many of those got a reply before 9 AM? | |
| How many of those customers never replied after your morning answer? | |
| Your usual reply time during the day (rough) | |

If many messages arrive after 9 PM and get answered the next morning, the rest of this guide is worth your time.

## 2. Free fixes in the WhatsApp Business app (30 minutes)

- [ ] **Business profile:** address, hours, website or catalogue link, short description.
- [ ] **Catalogue:** every product with price, sizes and a clear photo. Most night questions are "price?" and "available hai?"
- [ ] **Away message:** turn it on outside your hours. Say when you'll reply and link the catalogue.
  - Example: *"Shukriya! We're offline right now and will reply by 10 AM. Prices and sizes are in our catalogue: [link]. Leave your size and city and we'll confirm first thing."*
- [ ] **Greeting message:** for first-time chats, with the 2–3 things people ask most.
- [ ] **Quick replies:** `/price`, `/delivery`, `/cod`, `/sizes`, `/return`.
- [ ] **Labels:** New order, Waiting for payment, Confirmed, Shipped, Follow up.

The free app can't answer specific questions or take orders. That's where an agent comes in.

## 3. What a WhatsApp AI agent adds

An AI agent connected through the WhatsApp Business API (you need an approved setup) can:

- [ ] Answer price, size and stock questions from your real catalogue, in seconds, any time.
- [ ] Reply in Roman Urdu or English, matching the customer.
- [ ] Take the order: product, size, name, address, city, COD or prepaid.
- [ ] Send an order confirmation, so fewer COD parcels come back.
- [ ] Log every lead (name, number, question, time) to a sheet or CRM.
- [ ] Hand anything unusual (complaints, custom requests, bargaining) to you in the morning.

**Rules that keep it safe:**
- [ ] Only send business-started messages to people who opted in.
- [ ] Outside the 24-hour window after a customer's last message, only approved templates can be sent.
- [ ] Check Meta's current pricing for your country before you launch; costs change.
- [ ] Give the agent clear limits: no discounts or promises you haven't approved.

## 4. Night-shift order flow (example)

```
11:40 PM  Customer: "Bhai, available hai?"
11:40 PM  Agent:    Shares sizes in stock + price from the catalogue, asks "Order confirm karun?"
11:41 PM  Customer: "Haan, Large."
11:41 PM  Agent:    Collects name, address, city; confirms COD; sends order summary
 9:00 AM  You:      Review the overnight orders and dispatch
```
