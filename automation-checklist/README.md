# Stop Doing It by Hand: Automation Checklist

**If you do it the same way every day, software can probably do it for you.**

This guide lists 5 tasks most small businesses still do manually, and gives you a quick scorecard to find which of *your* tasks are worth automating first.

---

## 5 tasks most businesses still do by hand

| # | Task | By hand | Automated |
|---|------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | **Order & booking confirmations** | Typing the same "Got it, thanks!" message all day | Sent the moment someone books or orders |
| 2 | **Payment reminders** | Awkward follow-ups you keep putting off | Polite reminders on schedule that stop once paid |
| 3 | **The daily sales report** | Adding up the numbers every evening | Yesterday's numbers on your phone every morning |
| 4 | **Copying form data** | Retyping form answers into a spreadsheet | Forms feed straight into your system |
| 5 | **Appointment reminders** | Calling or messaging clients one by one | Automatic reminders sent the day before |

---

## Scorecard: is this task worth automating?

Pick one task you do regularly and score it. Give **1 point** for each statement that's true:

- [ ] I (or my team) do it **at least a few times a week**
- [ ] It follows **the same steps** every time
- [ ] It involves **copying information** from one place to another
- [ ] Mistakes or delays here **cost money or customers**
- [ ] It's **boring**, and people put it off

**4–5 points:** strong candidate, so automate this first.
**2–3 points:** worth looking at once the top ones are done.
**0–1 points:** probably fine as it is.

> Tip: list every repeating task for one week, score each one, and start with the highest score.

---

## Before you automate, write down

1. **Trigger:** what starts the task (a new order, a form, a date)?
2. **Steps:** what happens, in order?
3. **Where the data lives:** WhatsApp, email, spreadsheet, an app?
4. **Output:** what should happen at the end (a message, a report, a record)?

Those four answers are most of what a developer needs to scope the automation.
