# Cut Your COD Returns: Checklist + WhatsApp Templates

**Monday: new order! Friday: "maine order hi nahi kiya." Sound familiar?**

Cash-on-delivery returns quietly eat an online store's profit: you pay delivery both ways, and your stock is stuck in transit for a week. Industry estimates put typical COD return rates in Pakistan at **20–35%** ([source](https://trackmyorder.pk/blog/shopify-tips/cod-return-rate-pakistan-shopify)).

This checklist covers the simplest fixes, starting with confirming every order before you ship it.

---

## The fixes, in order of impact

- [ ] **Confirm every order on WhatsApp before dispatch.** Send it automatically within minutes of the order. No reply in 24 hours means the order goes on hold.
- [ ] **Send an "out for delivery" message** the morning the parcel goes out, so the customer is home and has cash ready.
- [ ] **Check the address and phone number** before shipping (city, area, landmark).
- [ ] **Keep a list of repeat refusers.** If someone refuses twice, call them first or ask for a small advance.
- [ ] **Encourage prepaid orders** with something small, such as faster dispatch or free delivery on prepaid.
- [ ] **Track your return rate monthly** to see what's working.

## Message templates (copy and adapt)

**1. Order confirmation (send automatically)**
> Assalam o Alaikum {name}! 👋 Thank you for your order #{order_id} from {store}.
> {items} · Total Rs {amount} (Cash on Delivery)
> Delivery to: {address}
> Reply **1** to confirm or **2** to cancel. We'll ship once you confirm. 🙏

**2. Reminder (if no reply after ~12 hours)**
> Hi {name}, just checking: should we ship your order #{order_id}? Reply **1** to confirm. If we don't hear back by tomorrow, we'll hold it.

**3. Out for delivery**
> Good news {name}! 🚚 Your order #{order_id} is out for delivery today. Please keep Rs {amount} ready. The rider may call from an unknown number.

---

## What a simple automation needs

- New order → confirmation message sent automatically
- Customer reply (1 / 2) → order marked **confirmed** or **cancelled**
- No reply → reminder, then **on hold**
- Daily list of confirmed orders ready to ship
- Repeat-refuser flag on the customer's record

This works with Shopify, WooCommerce, a custom store, or even orders taken in WhatsApp chats.
