# Customer Journey Map

**Map every step from "I saw your post" to "my order arrived", find the gates where customers get stuck, and turn your sales process into a direct flight.**

Every extra question a customer has to ask is a delay. And people don't like waiting: **70% of consumers expect a business to reply within an hour** ([EZ Texting, 2026 Consumer Texting Behavior Report](https://eztexting.com/report/2026-consumer-texting-behavior-report)), and **27% move on to a competitor rather than wait** ([Invoca B2C Buyer Experience Report 2026](https://www.marketingdive.com/press-release/20260608-new-invoca-study-finds-consumers-wont-wait-ai-gives-brands-the-speed-to-w/)).

## Step 3: Book the direct flight

| Gate | Layover version | Direct-flight version |
|------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| Price | "DM for price" | Prices on a website / catalog page, plus an AI agent that answers in seconds |
| Stock | "Let me check" | Stock shown live and updated on every sale |
| Delivery | "Will tell you" | Charges calculated automatically at checkout by area |
| Order | Long chat back-and-forth | An order button or a short form |
| Payment | "Send account number" | Online checkout or a payment link with the order |
| Confirmation | Manual message (sometimes) | Automatic confirmation message |
| Tracking | "Call me" | Tracking link sent automatically when it ships |

You don't need all of it on day one. Fix the busiest gate, then the next.
