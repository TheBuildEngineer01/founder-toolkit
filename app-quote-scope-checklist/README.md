# App Quote Scope Checklist

**Same app. $2,000 or $20,000?** Two honest developers can give wildly different quotes because they're pricing different apps.

This checklist helps founders and business owners get **comparable quotes** by describing the scope clearly before asking for a price.

> Prices above are illustrative, not a price list.

---

## The 5 things that change an app quote

| # | Area | Why it changes the price | Ask your developer |
|---|------|--------------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | **Screens & features** | "Simple" can mean 5 screens or 25. | Can you list every screen this price includes? |
| 2 | **Platforms** | Web only, Android only, or iOS + Android + web. | Which platforms does this quote cover? |
| 3 | **Admin side** | Managing users, orders and content is often a second app. | Is the admin panel included, and what can it do? |
| 4 | **Integrations** | Payments, WhatsApp, maps, your existing software. | Which integrations are in this price? |
| 5 | **After launch** | Hosting, updates and bug fixes. | What happens after launch, and what does it cost? |

---

## How to use it

1. Copy [`SCOPE_TEMPLATE.md`](./SCOPE_TEMPLATE.md).
2. Fill it in, even roughly.
3. Send the **same filled template** to every developer you're comparing.
4. Compare quotes line by line against the template, not just the total.
