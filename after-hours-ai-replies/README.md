# After-Hours AI Replies: Checklist + Templates

**11:04 PM: "Bhai ye available hai?" 9:30 AM: "Ji bilkul!" Too late. "Le liya kahin aur se."**

Customers message whenever they're free, often late at night. A WhatsApp-focused report found **73% of WhatsApp users say a slow reply stops them engaging with a brand at all** ([Gallabox, via Hyperleap 2026](https://hyperleap.ai/blog/smb-customer-messaging-expectations-2026)). An AI agent can answer the common questions in seconds, even while you sleep.

---

## Step 1: List what customers ask most

Look at your last 50 chats and count:

| Question | How often? | Can it be answered from a list or price sheet? |
|---|---|---|
| Price? | | ☐ yes |
| Available / in stock? | | ☐ yes |
| Delivery charges & time? | | ☐ yes |
| Sizes / colours / options? | | ☐ yes |
| Location / timings? | | ☐ yes |
| Booking / appointment? | | ☐ yes |
| Custom or complaint | | ☐ no, hand over to you |

Anything marked **yes** is a good job for an AI agent.

---

## Step 2: What the agent needs from you

- [ ] Product or service list with prices and stock (a spreadsheet is fine)
- [ ] Delivery areas, charges and times
- [ ] Business hours, location and contact
- [ ] Order or booking link (or the steps to take an order in chat)
- [ ] Tone: English, Roman Urdu, or both
- [ ] **Handover rule:** which questions should wait for a human

---

## Step 3: Reply templates (adapt to your business)

**Price & availability**
> Assalam o Alaikum! 👋 Yes, {product} is available in {options}. Price: Rs {price}. Delivery to {city} in {days} days, Rs {delivery}. Want me to book your order? Reply **YES**.

**Taking the order**
> Great! Please share: 1) your name, 2) full address with area, 3) phone number. I'll confirm your order right away.

**Handover to a human**
> Good question! I've passed this to {owner_name}. You'll get a reply first thing in the morning (after {opening_time}). Thank you for your patience. 🙏

**Follow-up if they go quiet (next day)**
> Hi {name}! Just checking: would you still like {product}? It's in stock right now. Reply **YES** and I'll book it for you.

---

## Rules that keep it safe and honest

- Only answer from **your** real price and stock list. Never let it guess.
- Make it easy to reach a human ("type HUMAN to talk to us").
- Don't spam: one follow-up, not five.
- Review a few chats every week and improve the answers.
